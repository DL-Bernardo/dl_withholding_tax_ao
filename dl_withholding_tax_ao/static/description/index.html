❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Straightforward answers to key architectural, fiscal, and licensing questions.

How does the 6.5% withholding tax calculation work in Angola?

Under Angolan tax legislation (Código do Imposto Industrial e Lei das Finanças Públicas), entities paying for technical or management services must retain 6.5% of the base taxable amount (excluding IVA) and remit it to the tax authority (AGT). The module automatically computes Withholding = Base × 6.5% and calculates Líquido a Pagar = (Base + IVA) - Withholding.

Does this module support both Vendor Bills and Customer Invoices?

Yes. While withholding tax is primarily retained by buyers on vendor bills (self-billing and supplier payments), the module also supports customer invoices when corporate or public entities apply withholding tax on payments made to your company.

Can I define custom tax rates besides 6.5%?

Yes. You have full freedom in Accounting → Configuration → Retenção na Fonte to create arbitrary tax rules, such as commercial property rental (15%), royalties (10%), or specialized non-resident brackets.

Is this module compatible with Odoo.sh?

Yes, 100%. The module is built strictly in Python and XML following official Odoo ORM guidelines with no external C-bindings or system dependencies, guaranteeing seamless deployment on Odoo.sh, Docker, or on-premise servers.

Does it conflict with standard Odoo accounting entries?

No. It uses standard non-intrusive model inheritance, cleanly managing retention lines without altering or breaking standard journal item validation workflows.
